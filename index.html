Her targets · 61.5 kg

Goal: build muscle and definition, not lose weight. That means eating to maintenance or a touch above, never under. At BMI ~19.5 there's nothing to cut.

Protein 120 g every day. The big lever. About 2 g/kg, which is right for an older vegan building muscle. Spread it across the day, roughly 30–40 g a meal, rather than backloading.

Carbs 230–250 g, up to ~270 on long-run and hard days to fuel and recover. Fat 60–70 g, weighted to omega-3s for the joints.

Around 2,050–2,200 kcal. Training days sit at the top of that. If a session feels flat, eat more, never train hungry.

Heart rate · age 50

Easy-run ceiling about 130 bpm. The whole plan is heart-rate led, not pace. The tracker flags a run in zone when avg HR stayed at or under 130, and too hard when it drifted above. Slower is fine. Walk breaks keep HR down on the long ones.

Before the mileage ramps

Ask the GP for a ferritin, vitamin D and B12 check. Low iron is the top reason women over 50 stall in training, and it's easily fixed. Only add an iron supplement if bloods come back low. Flag the new training load to the GP or rheumatology team if she's on RA medication.
